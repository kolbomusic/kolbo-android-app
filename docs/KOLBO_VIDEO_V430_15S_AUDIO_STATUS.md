# Kolbo Video 4.3.0 — 15 seconds with audio: release gates

## Confirmed in automated tests

- Real **moving input clips**, not static images: supports up to three genuine 1–5-second shots.
- Exact output 1–15 seconds from a 16 fps H.264 MP4 timeline.
- Audio muxing of owner-provided PCM WAV or genuine synthetic audio effects, with separate audio presence AND decoded audibility QA.
- Motion QA per clip and for final video, no missing shots padded or silent MP4 mislabelled as success.
- Final frame extracted between clips and passed to a verified model-provider callback for scene-continuation trials.
- No uncontrolled retries if a free provider accepts a submission with uncertain result.
- Owner-scoped, read-only `/v1/longform/plan` endpoint. Does not transfer media or spend GPU credits.
- Existing private/public AI routes are unchanged.
- The 15.000-second synthetic moving-video+AAC proof is **not AI footage**. Its FFmpeg testsrc2 video demonstrates a finished assembly workflow, *not generated acting*.

## NOT ready for public automatic 15s AI with voices

- The only demonstrated free WAN 2.2 endpoint makes a single 1–5s silent clip. Its current server trial cap is 2 submissions globally/day and 1 per installation/day. Our 15s segmented approach requires **three** verified submissions and a suitable model license. The server therefore advertises this as unavailable.
- Synthesized sound effects and user-supplied WAV are audible but **not** generated speech, singing, lip synchronization, or source-character performance.
- Three clips and last-frame continuation do **not** prove visual continuity or identical characters, and all videos still require content review.
- Render Free 512MB/low CPU is sufficient for video processing under limits, **not** neural generative video inference.
- On-device tests on the user's Android hardware and sustained availability on a Free provider are outstanding.
- No commercial model rights have been assumed. No payment instrument, advertiser, ad-network agreement or paid GPU has been enabled.

## Alternative native video+audio model

LTX-2.5 publishes an open-weight video-and-audio generation architecture with native multi-shot scene handling. This is a technical candidate for replacing stitched silent Wan outputs with actual synchronized video+sound generated by one model. **It is not deployed or validated in Kolbo Video** because available Free Render compute has no GPU and does not meet the model's requirements. Hardware and model licensing must be verified prior to enabling it.

Official docs: https://docs.ltx.io/open-source-model/getting-started/overview/

## Acceptance gates for a real Android release

1. A GPU/video+audio model produces an authorized, reference-consistent 15-second sample on controlled infrastructure.
2. Verifiable audio output includes the requested speech/song/sfx when requested and matches scene timing.
3. Tests confirm correct duration, visible motion, identity continuity and soundtrack alignment, not just codec presence.
4. A server-to-Android end-to-end run from a real handset succeeds with zero manual keys and without misleading fallbacks.
5. Provider use is authorized, free, bounded and does not automatically trigger paid services.
